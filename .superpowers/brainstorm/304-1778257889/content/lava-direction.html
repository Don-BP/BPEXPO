Directional Lava Brush — How should it flow?

You paint left→right. Which lava effect feels right?

A — Flowing Streams

Lava drips/streams extend in the direction of painting. Each particle has a glowing teardrop shape pointing forward — like lava oozing and spreading in the direction you move the brush.

B — Directional Blobs

Glowing lava blobs that are elongated/squashed in the direction of painting (like the rainbow band). The faster you paint, the more stretched they look. Scattered perpendicular to the stroke.
