Candy Cane Brush — Which approach?

Both follow the paint path. Pick the one that looks like what you have in mind.

A — Twisted Ribbon

A red-and-white ribbon that twists around the stroke path like a barber pole. The stripes rotate as the direction changes. Drawn as alternating angled wedges perpendicular to the stroke direction.

B — Spiral Wrap

The stripe spirals tightly around the path like thread on a spool. As you paint, the number of wraps you see depends on brush size. Works like a helical candy cane twist viewed from the side.
